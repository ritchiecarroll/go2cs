### 3.2 The per-row act, and all four overrides

Every row on every OS is ONE invocation of the pipeline, never the sweep (H10 step 1):

```
go2cs -tests -test-action all -test-config Release [-test-tiered] -test-timeout <T> -test-deadline <D> \
      [-test-build-maxcpu 4] -go2cspath <TREE>/src  <GOROOT>/src/<row>  <TREE>/src/core/<row>
```

- **The four overrides** (H10 step 1), with the rest of the preconditions table:

  | Variable | Value |
  |:--|:--|
  | `GOROOT` | `<GOROOT>` |
  | `GOTOOLCHAIN` | `local` |
  | `DOTNET_ROOT` | the .NET 10 root on any box whose machine SDK lags the TFM |
  | `PATH` | `<GOROOT>/bin` FIRST, then the .NET 10 root, then the rest: the `go` the converter spawns is the one PATH resolves (the preconditions table) |
  | `CGO_ENABLED` | `0`, exported (the corpus emission state) |

- **The output directory is the second positional** (floor 3), and it is the worktree whose `src/core` is the seed.
- **`<T>` and `<D>` are DERIVED, never copied.** `<T>` is `max(asked, $longTimeouts[row])` and `<D>` is
  `max(asked, $testDeadlineFloors[row])`, with asked = `30m` and `15m` (lesson 61).
- **`-test-tiered` exactly when the roster pins the row `release-tiered`.** At `5ee4bbbabd` those rows are
  `internal/godebug` and `net/http`, read from the roster rather than from lesson 68's list.
- **No `-tags`, no `-test-filter`, no `-test-allow-handown`.**
- **The rc is captured on the very next line**, with `2>&1` kept for the classifier.

**Windows lanes (i9, R-LAPTOP, H)** run the act through the recon wrapper, one process per shard list:

```
pwsh -NoProfile -File <SCRATCH>/run-h10-recon.ps1 -NameList <SCRATCH>/<shard>.txt -Tree <TREE> -GoRoot <GOROOT> \
     -Out <SCRATCH>/<shard>/<shard>.tsv -ExpectTip <FREEZE> -Scratch <SCRATCH>/<shard> -TestBuildMaxCpu 4
```

- The wrapper is a **per-run copy** of the `<FREEZE>` blob (floor 4), and `<TREE>` is a throwaway linked worktree
  detached at `<FREEZE>` (the recon leg's tree, never on a branch).
- `-TestBuildMaxCpu 4` is the `-m:4` the i9 and H measured under.
- A dotnet-tool `pwsh` gets `DOTNET_ROOT` INSIDE the child, never in the environment that launches it (the wrapper's
  header).

**Before row 1, on every windows lane:**
- run `run-h10-recon.ps1 -SelfTest -Tree <TREE> -NameList x -GoRoot x -Out x -ExpectTip x -Scratch x` under every
  shell the launcher uses, and require `SELF-TEST PASSED` (lesson 61);
- take the residue census, `git status --ignored=matching` with a never-built control, and record it;
- assert 25 GB free;
- grep a `DRIVER_EXIT=` marker rather than trusting `$LASTEXITCODE`.

**Linux lanes (R's WSL, P1, P2)** run the same act from a bash loop shaped like
`.claude/coord-scripts/templates/linux-legs.sh`:
- the clone checked clean (tracked AND untracked under tracked paths), then detached at `<FREEZE>`;
- `export GOROOT GOTOOLCHAIN=local CGO_ENABLED=0 GOFLAGS=` with `<GOROOT>/bin` first on PATH, and `GoTargetOS` NOT
  exported (the converter passes `-p:GoTargetOS` itself for a `-tests` row);
- one row at a time, the rc captured before any pipe;
- after each row, the row's dirt restored **by status, not by diff**, and build output purged;
- a free-space check at every row boundary.

The cloud lanes push their evidence at the END OF EACH SHARD (§3.4), because a container restart wipes tmpfs, as
measured: P2 lost an 80-minute arm to one on 2026-10-23.

**The resume ledger is the shard's.** One line per row, append-only, keyed on the corpus commit, the converter commit and
the converter binary's mtime (§3.4). A restarted lane re-enters at its first unrecorded row and **re-dispatches a
killed row at a RAISED budget**, never the same one (§3.6).

### 3.4 How a lane reports: ONE post per shard

To COORD, at most 40 lines (§7 lesson 82): WHAT (one line), EVIDENCE (up to ten), and ASK or NEXT (one line).

- **WHAT:** the lane, the shard, `<FREEZE>`, and the lane ref and SHA, read back by `ls-remote`.
- **EVIDENCE:**
  - the toolchain three ways (PATH `go version`, the resolved binary, `GOROOT`'s `VERSION`), with the .NET version;
  - the rows run against the rows listed;
  - the word tally (`PASS`, `DIVERGED`, `CONVERT`, `BUILD`, `TIMEOUT`, `NOVERDICT`);
  - every non-PASS row by name, with its cause;
  - every `TIMEOUT` with its results-file tail line (floor 14);
  - "failed with named verdicts" kept apart from "failed with none" (§3.4);
  - the residue census before and after;
  - the largest `post_s`.
- **ASK or NEXT:** what the lane runs next, or the ruling it needs.

A NOVERDICT row is never a 0: it is `NOMATCH` with its cause (H10, THE WORDS).
