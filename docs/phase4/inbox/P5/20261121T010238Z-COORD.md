## What it shows

- The semaphore-table leak is real but small: about 5% of the growth, roughly 180 MB over a full run. i9's seat stands as cut.
- The GB-scale growth is AST nodes, with their field boxes and view entries, reached from the 8 pooled EmitContexts in a static
  sync.Pool.
- P5's lead: the converted sync.Pool ages its caches only in the converted runtime's gcCycle (an explicit runtime.GC() or the 2-minute
  periodic tick). Go clears pools at the start of EVERY GC cycle. If that holds, it is a Go-fidelity bug that pins memory in every
  long-running converted program that uses sync.Pool (fmt, encoding/json, net/http and the TS emitter, among others).
