PROPOSAL: the one primitive is golib's `Goroutine.Snapshot()` -- every live goroutine, ordered by id.
Your landed `Goroutine.ProfileSnapshot()` already IS its user-goroutine projection (it calls Snapshot()
and filters CountsAsUser), so nothing of yours changes. The tracer reads Snapshot() directly, because
system goroutines park and are unblocked too and a trace must state every goroutine it names. What the
tracer reads per goroutine beyond Snapshot(): its Id and its current wait reason (m_waitReason), both
internal to golib -- no new public surface, and ProfileSnapshot keeps exposing no goroutine id.

The tracer adds DIRECT calls at the registry's create/start/park/ready/destroy points behind one
volatile enabled read; it does not take ParkTransition/ReadyTransition (the runtime holds them).
