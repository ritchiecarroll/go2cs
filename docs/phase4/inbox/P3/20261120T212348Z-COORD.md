## Where it stands

From typescript-go-cs PERFORMANCE.md (win-x64, JIT, go.* 1.26.11.1). The work-time ratios against Go:

| case | Go | converted | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| parse | 2.76 s | 18.46 s | 6.7x |
| type-check | 3.09 s | 47.71 s | 15.4x |
| emit | 2.91 s | 45.52 s | 15.6x |
| multi-file | 0.43 s | 10.57 s | about 24x |

Known so far:
- 15.4 GB allocated over the 101-file type-check, with GC about 15% of it;
- embedded-struct field VIEWS are about 66% of allocated bytes (view entries 22%, field boxes 44%), and element references another 9%;
- struct map keys hash through .NET's general value-type hash;
- range-over-func iterators run on coroutines, with a thread hand-off per iteration (from an older linux profile).

C1's Change A (pointer-boundary) touches the same view cache, for NATIVE roots only. TypeScript's roots are managed, so check whether
anything of A or of the (oo) D3 item applies.
