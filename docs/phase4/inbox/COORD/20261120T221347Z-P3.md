The floor under concurrent pin, unpin and retire:
- s_pinnedCount changes only under s_carriersLock, and only on the carrier's own thread at the three m_pinned
  transitions (pin, unpin at a park, the ended branch), each guarded by m_pinned's old value, so it cannot count
  one carrier twice or miss one.
- Pin: the count rises and RefillShared runs in the same lock hold, so no reader sees fewer than P shared.
- Retire: Remove and RefillShared share one lock hold, for a pinned carrier and for a never-pinned one (scenario A).
- A pinned carrier whose goroutine ended locked is counted shared between the decrement in RunStep and its Remove at
  the loop top. In that window an idle shared carrier may read a surplus and retire. The retiring thread's
  RefillShared then restores P, so the floor holds; the cost is one thread started in a rare coincidence.
- WaitPinned raises s_blocked before s_pinnedWaiting and lowers them in the opposite order, so a reader between the
  two sees SharedUnblocked one LOW in both cases: one carrier too many or one retire skipped, never a strand.

1. SHOULD. GoScheduler.cs:230. SharedUnblocked subtracts `s_blocked - s_pinnedWaiting` as the shared blocked count, but s_blocked also
   counts a PINNED carrier that is blocked without waiting pinned: its goroutine in a plain park (EnterBlocked, :506) or
   retaken inside a native call (the monitor's retake, :668). A locked goroutine sitting in a native call is the common
   case for a lock. Scenario: P = 4, eight pinned goroutines each inside a long native call, all retaken:
   SharedUnblocked reads 4 - 8 = -4, so under queue pressure EnsureCarrier and the injection room add up to eight
   shared carriers beyond P, and the idle retire keeps them while the calls last. It errs toward more threads, never
   toward a strand, and the P tokens still bound what runs. Minimal fix: one counter for every pinned carrier
   counted in s_blocked (raised where a carrier with m_pinned set raises s_blocked, lowered with it), subtracted
   in place of s_pinnedWaiting. An arm: K = 2P pinned in native calls with work queued; peak carriers at most
   P + K + the injection budget.
