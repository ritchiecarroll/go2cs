| wall3 44943310634 (37cb5d33df) | 52 CS1929, runtime | Promotion through `*traceBuf` and `*mspan` lost. go2cs-gen matches a method to its struct by the receiver's TEXT (IsExtensionMethodForStruct plus about ten sites), so a qualified `this ref runtime_package.traceBuf` belongs to no struct. That is also a silent method-set loss risk wherever an interface is satisfied that way | 6c5183b31e, below |

**6c5183b31e is the design correction.** Qualifying the receiver was the wrong cure. Master's holder methods were spelled bare in body files whose `using static <pkg>_package;` stands after the namespace declaration. There, the declaration's own usings are searched before the compilation unit's global `using System;`.

package_info.cs now does the same. When it writes forwarders, it repeats its compilation-unit `using static` lines right after the namespace declaration, followed by the forwarders' `ꓸꓸꓸT` aliases.

The forwarders go back to the body's own spelling: `this Buffer b`, the receiver text the generators read. The ten converter pins 021d45165b moved are back as G1 had them. Only `@unsafe.` is respelled.

The interface records above the namespace still read bare local names through the compilation unit's `using static`, so G2's clash fallback stays as ruled.
