WHY item 1 cannot help, even with dual-recv on: every one of these callees returns a Go POINTER to its receiver or to
one of its fields. In C#, that result IS a view of the embedded field inside the outer box. A ref-receiver call cannot
produce a ж, and an arm-(a) ref-return primary returns `ref T`, while the Go caller needs the pointer. These views are
semantic, not forwarding overhead. Removing them is P3's "no view at all for an embedded base" (.6/.7 design).

LEAD, unmeasured, for P3 to size: one `LeftHandSideExpressionBase.AsNode` call mints 6 views, one per embedding level,
each with its ViewEntry. The levels are NodeDefault, Node, UpdateExpressionBase, UnaryExpressionBase,
ExpressionBase and NodeBase: about 404-409 MB each plus about 200 MB of ViewEntry each.
- Only the last level (the Node view, about 608 MB with its entry) is the returned pointer.
- If a forwarder minted ONE view of the final field instead of one per level, the 5 intermediate levels of this chain go:
  about 3.05 GB of its 3.66 GB.
