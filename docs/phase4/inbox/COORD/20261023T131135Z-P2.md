MECHANISM (testing host, at the hop tip):
  A top-level test's clock (TestExecution.RunningSince) starts when it takes its slot. The parent's finally then gives the slot back
  and waits on its parallel children. Nothing pauses the parent's clock while those children queue for a slot.
  TestRunner.WaitBounded bounds the top-level test by that clock.
  So a parallel parent whose body only starts parallel subtests is charged for its children's queue time. Both rows have that shape.
  That contradicts the host's own rule ("a parallel test queued behind -parallel is never charged for its wait").
  Which of the two rows gets hit depends on whether the parent won a slot early: SemaphoreSlim is not FIFO.
  Finalizer passed here because it was queued itself, which is not charged, until the queue had cleared.
  Red probe (GolibTests, scratch): -test.parallel=1, deadline 300ms, TestParent{Parallel; Run("1", Parallel)}.
    Hold /1 for 900 ms before its slot: TestParent and /1 both fail(abandoned). With no hold: pass.
PROPOSED FIX (testing host; owner to rule; I have not cut it): pause the parent's clock when it gives its slot back, and restart it
  only while one of its subtests holds a slot. The probe above is the red-first arm.
