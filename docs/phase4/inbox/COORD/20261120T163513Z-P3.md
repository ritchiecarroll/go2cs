1. MUST-FIX the untyped placement. The design puts A "at the miss in the untyped viewOf". The miss is after
   tryFindView (ж.Views.cs:156), and tryFindView walks viewsHead(), which for a NativeBox is
   s_views.GetOrCreateValue(this) (:144): the weak-table entry, its lock and the ViewTable are created before the miss
   is known. The untyped path would keep the largest cost A removes, and the red arm stays red there.
   Fix: in both overloads the arm runs BEFORE tryFindView (typed: top of viewOfSlow, as proposed; untyped: before :156).
   This also answers open question (2): take the untyped overload, same arm, same place.

2. MUST-FIX one body, one guard. Today the test is `m_source is INativeRooted { NativeRootAddress: not 0 }` and
   GoFieldOffsetOf(m_source, m_token) >= 0 (ж.FieldRefBox.cs:310). The design words it as "a non-nil NativeBox<T>".
   Fix: the new static takes (source, token) and holds the whole of :310 and :330 (address test, offset, array
   window, Kept); FieldRefBox.NativeFieldBox calls it with (m_source, m_token), and A calls it with (this, token) under
   `this is NativeBox<T>`. Then the direct answer and the view-derived answer cannot differ, and the token A passes
   must be the ORIGINAL accessor (what m_token holds, :127), not the wrapper.
   Scenario if they drift: one field answers at two addresses, by of() on the root and by a hop that still builds
   a FieldRefBox, and equality by address fails.

3. SHOULD-FIX keep the lazy flip. tryFindView sets BoxShape<T>.Slotted on the first of() of any ж<T>
   (ж.Views.cs:223). With A ahead of it, a T whose first of() is on a native root no longer flips, so managed boxes
   and array element boxes of T minted afterwards stay on the unslotted route until a managed miss. Answers are
   the same (the file's own note says so) but allocation counts and cost placement move, and banked allocation
   rows read them. Fix: A's arm sets the flag as tryFindView does, or the cut states the move and shows
   AllocationCounterTests and the alloc rows unchanged.

4. SHOULD-FIX name what stays on today's route, in the design and as an arm: a nil root, an unknown layout, the
   cache disabled, and a native root that is not a NativeBox<T> (any other INativeRooted with a root address).
   Each must still answer exactly as master; the identity arm should include one of each.
