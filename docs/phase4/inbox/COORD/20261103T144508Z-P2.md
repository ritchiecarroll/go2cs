# FLAG: P2's conditional purge has no hook in the frozen kit, and a 25G trigger from a 25G start fires every row; proposal: kit floor as the trigger at 18G
from: P2 · to: COORD · 2026-11-03T14:45:08Z

FLAG before the re-pass dispatch: P2's conditional purge (repass-briefs.md, P2 block, "BEFORE EACH ROW, only if free
< 25 GB") has (1) no hook in the frozen kit, and (2) a trigger that fires on almost every row from a 25G start.

(1) MECHANISM. rebank-linux.sh's row loop is closed: after a row it restores, checks free >= FLOOR_GB, and starts the
next row in the same instant. A purge cannot be placed between rows without editing the kit, and a purge DURING a
row would delete the dependency build output that row is building on. The only seam is the kit's own floor check:
  - run the kit with FLOOR_GB = the purge trigger; its row-boundary ABORT ("free XG below NG before <row>") IS the
    "before this row, free < trigger" test, at the right place, by the kit's own df -BG;
  - then P2's wrapper (outside the kit) purges git clean -fdXq -- src/core, asserts no ' D', re-reads free, logs
    (row, free before/after), STOPS as a floor abort if free < 15, else re-launches the kit at the first unrecorded
    row (resume LIST, same SHARD and evidence dir; each relaunch's rows carry their converter mtime);
  - the launch preflight (kit line 50) then also needs free >= FLOOR_GB.
  The 15 GB row floor is then the wrapper's post-purge check, not the kit's. No kit byte changes.

(2) PREDICTION from P2's own discovery logs (per-row free after restore, kit df -BG):
  - P2-1: 21G at launch, 19G after row 1, then -0.2G per row: 18 at row 2, 16 at row 14, 15 at row 17, 13 at row 19.
  - P2-2: 23G at launch, 21G after row 1, 18 by row 4, 15 by row 18, 15 at row 23.
  - A launch's FIRST row costs ~2G (cold dependency builds); later rows ~0.2G (warm).
  So with a 25G start and a 25G trigger: free after row 1 is ~23G and the purge fires before row 2. Each purge makes
  the next row cold again (-2G), so it fires before nearly EVERY row. That breaks the warm-wall prediction, and your
  1-in-4 rule stops P2 at row 4 of 12.
PROPOSAL: trigger = floor + 3 = 18G (FLOOR_GB=18 in the kit, the wrapper's 15G floor after a purge). From the logs,
  a 46-row shard from 25G (-2, then -0.2 x 45, about -11) would purge once, near row 27. That is under your
  1-in-4 rule, and the warm walls hold.
  Or keep 25 and expect the rebalance at row 4.
Your call. Nothing runs before the dispatch; the wrapper is written and checked only once you rule.
