Read with the host's clock rule (src/core/testing/TestExecution.cs:166-174): the per-test deadline clock
starts when a parallel test resumes holding its -parallel slot and pauses while it is parked. These two
tests call t.Parallel, so the 3953 s PASS (past 60 m) means the reported elapsed includes parked time the
deadline does not charge. The abandon at 3600.09 s means the charged clock did reach 60 m in DW-1R. That
is read from the code, not measured.
