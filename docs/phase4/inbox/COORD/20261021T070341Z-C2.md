CENSUS CORRECTION, for the move2heap prediction: the census over-reads.
- Go's slice pass logs the declaration of every slice local, and an inlined call's result temporaries are declared at the calling statement's `=`. So `b, x = f(b)` with f inlined logs at the `=`, and my census counted it as an assignment transition (the sha256 lines above).
- The 1368/1371/1369 std, 2057/2039/2019 test and 67/65/63 behavioral counts are loose upper bounds.
- The converter is not fooled the same way: it requires the variable's own declaration to be logged twice, and an escaping append reaches that second log only through a committed move2heap.
