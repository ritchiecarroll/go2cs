WHAT S5 NEEDS THAT MASTER LACKS: the arm leg is only meaningful once S1b (GoTargetArch through
-tests and run-validated-sweep), S2 (the linux/arm64 flavour) and S6 (a pack that ships
runtimes/linux-arm64) exist. Before S2, an arm leg builds the linux/AMD64 flavour, which makes amd64
system calls on an arm64 host. So I propose three cuts, each landing when its input does:

S5a (now, about 0.5 day): the plan gains a linux-arm64 leg (runner ubuntu-24.04-arm, rid linux-arm64,
  env GoTargetArch) behind an OPT-IN input, `arch` = x64 (default) | arm64 | both, honoured for
  goos=linux only. Existing dispatches are byte-for-byte unchanged. Stage budgets get an arm factor,
  measured not guessed: one census and one golib-tests run on ubuntu-24.04-arm against TODAY's amd64
  flavour, labelled "calibration, not the arm64 flavour". That gives the first real arm walls (the
  design's 18 min / 10-13 min are linux-x64 estimates) and a recorded pre-S3 GolibTests reading on
  the arm64 ABI: S3's red baseline, which nobody has yet. The leg refuses sweep-shard,
  behavioral-* and the smokes until a flavour marker exists (S2), so it cannot bank a wrong-ABI row.
S5b (after S1b + S2, about 0.5-1 day): the leg passes GoTargetArch=arm64 through census,
  golib-tests, behavioral-* and sweep-shard (no -test-oracle-goarch: the host IS arm64); `arch`
  defaults to both for goos=linux, as darwin fans out to two runners; a shard plan for the arm sweep
  derived from the linux bank's measured walls (rebank-1.26.10.3/linux/*.tsv): 4+ shards under the
  6 h job cap, runtime (1945 s on linux-x64) in its own job, timing-sensitive rows re-measured on arm.
S5c (after S6, about 0.5 day): release-smoke and aot-smoke at five RIDs (aot-smoke ON the arm runner,
  per F6); RidCompileAsset's -UnshippedRid moves off linux-arm64 (SEAT 0's arm refuses by name once
  go.syscall ships it); docs/CIMatrix.md.
