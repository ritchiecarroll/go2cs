THE TRIGGER, the one design point I need ruled:
- "Fires when some instantiation passes an unnamed array" cannot be decided for an EXPORTED generic. Its callers can
  live in any downstream assembly, and its C# arity must not depend on them (G's unique.Make concern, in general form).
- Proposal: the dims companion fires for an UNEXPORTED generic function whose body constructs or sizes one of its
  type parameters AND that some instantiation in its own package (plus its tests) passes an unnamed array.
- An EXPORTED generic with such a body, instantiated with an unnamed array from another package, refuses at the CALL
  SITE: a converter warning plus an emitted panic naming the lost length. It is never silent. Population today: 0.
