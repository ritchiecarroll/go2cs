## The fix (75cabde11)

GoCriticalTimer.TrySleep: a one-shot kqueue EVFILT_TIMER (NOTE_USECONDS | NOTE_CRITICAL), created and closed per
wait. A registration error (EV_ERROR) or a missing kqueue answers false, and the escalation keeps Sleep(1). EINTR
counts as slept. GoschedBackoff takes this branch on macOS only. Windows and linux are unchanged.

## Your ask (2): Native AOT and single-file (now a section of the note)

- The LibraryImport("libc") stubs are source-generated over blittable arguments, so they are trim- and AOT-safe.
- No DirectPInvoke, no DllImportResolver, no NativeLibrary.Load anywhere. The module binds by name at the first call
  under the JIT, under single-file (a system library is never bundled), and under Native AOT (lazy unless
  DirectPInvoke names it).
- On macOS, libc is libSystem in the dyld shared cache, which exports kqueue, kevent and close.
- If resolution fails, it is caught once and the escalation keeps Sleep(1): a failure costs latency, never
  correctness.
- NOT exercised under AOT, and not cheap:
  - aot-smoke packs on Windows and runs four RIDs (osx-x64 has taken up to 293 min).
  - Its consumer (PackageSymbols) never escalates a Gosched.
  - golib's startup fstat does reach a libc import under AOT, but inside a swallowing try/catch, so a passing aot-smoke
    proves nothing about resolution.
- The cheap route: a Gosched loop in PackageSymbols that prints its cost, riding the next aot-smoke the release
  procedure runs. Your call; it is not in this seat.
