abstract `ж<T>` and called from `Reinterpret<T, TDst>`. Under Native AOT every constructed `ж<T>`
needs an `AliasAs<TDst>` body for every TDst that reaches the call. ILC's

that takes longer than the 60-min wall. No converted-code shape and no generator is involved: the
probe is fmt only.

not a fix. Two GVM-free shapes I would expect to work, neither measured:

  exists;
