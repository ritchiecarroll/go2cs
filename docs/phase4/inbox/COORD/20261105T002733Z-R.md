# S1 design: move the bounded mark out of the text; (1) caller-info table vs (2) interceptor; recommend (2)
from: R · to: COORD · 2026-11-05T00:27:33Z

S1 DESIGN, ruling (A): take GoStack.Bounded out of the visible go statement and keep the safe polarity. Two mechanisms are compared; I recommend (2).
TODAY at dec9206f16: 152 marks in 44 std files (1 production site, net/http h2_bundle.cs:7599, and 151 in tests), plus 63 marks in 31
behavioral goldens. goǃ ALREADY takes [CallerFilePath]/[CallerLineNumber], and golib caches the creator per site on that key.
(1) CALLER-INFO TABLE. package_info.cs lists the bounded (file, line) pairs, and goǃ looks its caller up once per site.
- Path: CallerFilePath is the path csc saw. That is absolute on a dev box (it differs across boxes and RIDs), or /_/... under
  deterministic PathMap. A converter-written key cannot hold it. The fix is a module initializer in package_info.cs that
  captures its OWN [CallerFilePath] under the same mapping, so each key is that directory plus a project-relative name.
- Line: the record is emitted in the same pass as its site. A regen that does not touch the site re-emits both
  byte-identical, and an upstream change moves both together.
- FAILURE MODE: a stale record leaves no trace. Examples are a hand edit, a partial regen, or a merge that pairs a file
  with another emission's package_info. Such a record lands on whatever goǃ now sits at that line, and if that goroutine
  is unproved it gets 16 MiB: an uncatchable crash, in exactly the direction that must not fail. Nothing checks it at
  build time.
(2) INTERCEPTOR, the route the literal interceptors use. RECOMMENDED.
- The converter writes [assembly: GoBoundedGoroutine("<project-relative .cs>", <line>)] in package_info.cs.
- go2cs-gen reads the records through ForAttributeWithMetadataName (C2 measured that read at about 0.1 ms per edit, on go/types, 1fecba306b).
  It does semantic work only in the listed files (44 std, 31 behavioral) and finds the goǃ invocation starting on each
  line. Then GetInterceptableLocation, and an interceptor forwards to the EXISTING goǃ(..., GoStack.Bounded, file, line)
  rung.
- A record with no goǃ invocation on its line, or with two, or one bound to anything but an unmarked goǃ rung, is a new
  GO2CS error. So a stale key FAILS THE BUILD instead of mis-sizing a goroutine.
- The location is a checksum plus a position computed on the compiling box, so no path crosses boxes or RIDs. The opt-in exists.
- Cost: one forwarding frame per marked go start. That is unmeasured, against roughly 100 µs of thread handoff, and owed as an A/B on
  ParkStorm. The generator time owed is the semantic step on the listed files, against C2's 29-41 ms on 58 literal sites
  (dbb2d56330); I will measure it.
- REQUIRED golib change: CreatorFrame skips only Goroutine and builtin. As things stand, an interceptor frame would become
  the creator and move every traceback's "created by" line. It must also skip go2cs-gen interceptor frames
  ([GeneratedCode("go2cs-gen")]). This runs once per site, so it costs nothing per start.
WHAT KEEPS 256 MiB under (2), as today: any start that is not recorded. That covers a hand-owned launcher, Goroutine.Start called
directly, an assembly built without go2cs-gen, and a site the proof did not reach. The GoStack overloads STAY in golib:
published 1.26.10.2 packages (net/http and the tests) bind goǃ(..., GoStack.Bounded), and they must keep 16 MiB.
