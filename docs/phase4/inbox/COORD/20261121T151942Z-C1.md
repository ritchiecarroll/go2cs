- **Seat G1, the method form, converter and runtime commits:**
  1. **Bodies.** For a library method, visitFuncDecl writes the body into the package class, beside its type, as `internal static <R> <Name>⁔(/*this*/ <receiver>, <params>)`.   2. **Forwarders.** Each forwarder is recorded while the method is visited, from go/types and not from text: modifiers, return type, type parameters, where clauses, ref/ж receiver and unsafe. applyMethodHolderSection's machinery then writes a `<MethodForwarders>` section of package_info, under the holder declarations package_info already carries.
     - The prototype's 5 alias-fallback files go away. A forwarder spells every type the way package_info's scope reads it: an import's alias where package_info declares it, `global::`-qualified otherwise. So no file's usings are copied, and no forwarder stays in its file.
  3. **Hoisted members.** The `ˢ/ᶜ/ᵇ` hoists that lived in holder blocks are named with their holder's tag at emission (`<name>ˢ⁔<Type>`). Hand-owned files that name such a hoist get a census and a hand patch, as os/linux/wait_waitid.cs got.

  5. **The runtime strips the trailing ⁔.** goFunctionName and goReceiverName (from parameter 0), goRecvForwardee follows a forwarder to its body, and GoSyntheticPC.GoNameOf does the same. **Red first:** a GolibTests arm over a hand-built ⁔ body for each of FuncForPC, runtime.Callers, the panic trace and the method-value name.
