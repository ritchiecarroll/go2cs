restores the newest 1.26 release. Or set GoStdLibVersion to 1.26.* in your Directory.Build.props yourself. Your
current 1.26.5.* also restores, but NuGet resolves it to the lowest release above 1.26.5 (1.26.10.1 today), and it
will not move to later 1.26 releases.
