ROOT CAUSE: the converter. Not go2cs-gen, and not the package_info writer (it only qualifies the names as
strings). The fault is renderCSFullTypeName in src/go2cs/typeNameResolution.go.
- The generic-argument pass ran BEFORE the pointer branch and the two func branches, and rewrote the
  arguments to C# in place. Those branches then re-render their pieces: the pointer's element, each func
  parameter and result.
- So a second pass received C# text. Most arms produce the same output on their own output. The `map<` arm
  does not: splitMapKeyValue splits `@string, @string>` at its first top-level `>`, keeping the whole list

- Measured at master: `*Expected[map[string]string]` -> `ж<Expected<map<@string, @string, >>>`, and `**` adds two.
  Broken the same way: func(Expected[...map...]), func(x ...) error, func() Expected[...map...],
  *Outer[Inner[map...]], *Pair[map,map].
  Correct before the fix: plain Expected[map], []/chan/*[] of it, *map, *Expected[string].
- The "join" is not the cause. It is the re-render after the join.

REPRO: my own, at 38caf3558a: a generic struct over a map, cast via a pointer to an interface. The record
reads `[assembly: GoImplement<Expected<map<@string, @string, >>, TypeValidatedField>(Pointer = true)]`, with the
non-nested `Expected<@string>` record beside it as the control. When the TS repro lands I'll check it reads

inside their recursion, once per piece. For every other name the order of operations is unchanged.
- Side effect, a correction rather than a defect: the Δ-collision rename in that pass compared the base `*T`,
