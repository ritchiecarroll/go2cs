## D2: APPROVE WITH CHANGES if the census justifies building it; the RACE 5 repair alone is not enough
Answer to (a): three repairs are needed, not one.
1 MUST-FIX registrant side, PT:546. The registrant holds W from its TryGetValue. Sequence: remover compare-removes
  W, re-reads the target, still dead, decrements; then the registrant calls W.SetTarget(box) and returns. W is in
  no table: the registration is lost for good, not for a window. Fix: after SetTarget the registrant confirms the
  table still maps the address to W; if not, it adds (and counts) as for a new address.
2 MUST-FIX Promote, PT:809. "Promote is not touched" does not hold. Promote(token, failed) runs after the reader
  saw W dead (PT:678) or alive but not pinned (PT:704), and then does TryUpdate(address, candidate, W). If W was
  revived in between, the compare passes and the new box is replaced by an older registrant; the new box is in
  neither the table nor the kept list, and its number stops resolving when that older one dies. Today a new
  registration is a new instance, the compare fails, and the comment at PT:807 relies on it. Fix: after a
  successful TryUpdate, re-read W; if it is alive and pinned there, KeepDisplaced(address, W).
3 SHOULD-FIX the repaired RACE 5 window is a new state, not today's. Today a MISS on a pinned address needs the
  read to precede the registration's return. With the repair, RegisterPinned has returned, the box is alive and
  pinned, and a third reader between the remove and the TryAdd back still gets MISS, which callers read as
  "native": the wrong-arm direction, however short. Either show no caller acts irreversibly on one MISS, or accept
  D2 only where the census shows the saving is worth that state.
4 NIT RACE 4's reason is wrong though its outcome stands. Two live boxes can be pinned at one address: PT:709
  says every evaluation of &b[0] mints a fresh box and each registers the same address. The parity argument is
  that today's indexer store loses the same simultaneous registrant; say that instead.

address resolves after RegisterPinned returns; (ii) the Promote case with one kept registrant; (iii) run both
against the unrepaired SetTarget form first and show them fail.
