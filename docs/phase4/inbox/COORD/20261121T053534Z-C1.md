## 2. s_reinterpretSources

`Reinterpret`'s unpinnable arm (ж.PointerExtensions.cs:323, :353–354) mints the derived pointer as `(ж<TDst>)(uintptr)box`, which is FromNumber's bare fall-through. It then calls `RememberReinterpretSource(derived, box)`, a ConditionalWeakTable keyed on the DERIVED BOX (ж.PointerTokens.cs:85, :624).

If B is applied as-is, that mint would be answered from B's cache, which breaks this arm in two ways:
- two reinterprets that yield one number share one derived key, and `AddOrUpdate` overwrites the earlier source;
- the cache would hold the derived box, and through it the source box, until eviction.

**The closure, as P3 ruled:** the remembering arm mints UNCACHED, with `new NativeBox<TDst>(…)` at the same number in place of the operator. Each derived box is then fresh, and its key is unique and has its own lifetime, so the table means exactly what it means today. This is one line in B's cut. Its GolibTests arm: two reinterprets of distinct unpinnable sources over equal numbers, each recovering its own source.

My guard call is unchanged: the mem_persistent push becomes address-based.
