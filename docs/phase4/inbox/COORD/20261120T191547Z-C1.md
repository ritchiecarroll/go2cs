     "<file>:<line> <name>"), and the wrapper counts the site.

   - SITE is still sampled; it now names only the CALLER. It is not scored.
2. DISTINCT is capped PER TYPE: 16,000,000 by default, GO2CS_VIEW_CENSUS_DISTINCT_CAP to change it.

     first run's floor of 1 M distinct over all types suggests far less.
3. BARE, new, per T: mints that keep nothing (no pin, no retained source, not an order-token carrier). This is

## P3's three readings (a)-(c), for the design ruling
(a) One root type carries it: 609,498 of 729,562 (83.5%) are NativeBox<sqlite3 mutex>. Agreed, and so is P3's
    consequence: on a workload without that pattern the falsifier would fire. Change A is general (any native root
    with a known layout) and costs one type test per slow call. Its gain is proportional to how often a program
    takes `&x.f` or a pointer-receiver method of a field through a freshly converted pointer. In sqlbench that is
    mostly one type.
(b) Change A leaves 275,702 weak-live lookups (StandardBox and StaticBox). Agreed: those are not A's. They are FOUND
    entries, a table lookup each and not a create, on kinds without a slot (StaticBox, and StandardBox minted before
    its type's lazy flip). After A they are nearly all of SLOW. I name them as a separate candidate, not part of A:
    give StaticBox the slot, or flip earlier. G's profile after A decides whether they are worth a seat.
(c) Mints are 93 times the field views, and B may pay after all. Agreed, and my 163124Z expectation ("I expect B not
    to be worth it") was WRONG by the first condition. MINT/DISTINCT of 20,018 to 488,751 for the three struct types
    meets it by orders of magnitude. The wrong expectation stays visible where I posted it.
    - B's second condition is how many of those mints are BARE; census-2 measures that.
    - B's third is its own design read after S-B1 and wall C, because both read a box's retained source and B
      changes box identity.
    - Revised expectation: if B1 holds, B pays on sqlbench's struct types, and B becomes the larger change after A.
