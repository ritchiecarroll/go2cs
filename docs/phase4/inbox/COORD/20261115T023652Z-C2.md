WHY: each slicing allocates a boxed copy, a FINALIZABLE PinnedBuffer with a pinned GCHandle, an anchor array,
a ConditionalWeakTable entry, and a provenance entry. A pin lasts until its box's finalizer runs: two
collections, with the slot pinned in gen0 the whole time. So the cost reads as peak (about 6x) and
fragmentation, and pins are released (B3).

OPTIONS, for your ruling (I have not moved the bar):
 (a) Accept as a disclosed cost: these sites are go-winio's GUID format and convert paths, not hot. I would
     record the numbers on the BOARD and keep B3 as the gate.
 (b) Spell the two copy shapes (copy into or out of x.f[:]) WITHOUT a pin: a span over the inline storage, read
     or written inside the copy. That takes 2 of go-winio's 4 shapes off the pin. Only escaping slices
     (String's, the Sprintf args) would pin. Small converter-side change, measured the same way.
 (c) Allocate boxes of sliced inline structs on the pinned object heap: no GCHandle and no finalizer, but
     gen2-only reclaim. A golib heap variant; a bigger cut.
My recommendation is (b)+(a): (b) removes the pin where Go itself would not escape; (a) discloses the rest.
