SHOULD-FIX
1. RuntimeSemaphore.cs, Acquire's `b.Waiters.Enqueue(w)`, with internal/sync/runtime_impl.cs:115-120. `lifo` is still dropped and the queue is FIFO only. At the base that was a stated fairness limit, with the reason "starvation mode still works, because Semrelease's handoff reaches the primitive": the handoff happened under the bucket lock and could not fail. This cut makes the handoff conditional, as Go's is, without Go's other half. Case: a Mutex in starvation mode, waiters W1..Wn queued, new goroutines arriving. Unlock hands off; a newcomer's lock-free cansemacquire takes the permit first; W1 is readied, fails, and re-queues at the TAIL behind W2..Wn. Go re-queues it at the HEAD (queueLifo, because waitStartTime != 0). So the longest waiter can be passed over once per round, which neither Go nor the base allows. Mutual exclusion holds; the wait bound does not. Fix: honour lifo (a deque, add at the head when lifo is set) and pass it from both runtime_impl files; or keep FIFO and rewrite that comment to say what is now true, with a BOARD row.
