## ASK 1: a Goexit inside the chain. It FOUND A GoFrame DEFECT, now fixed (f200c8cfd0)
- Pinned on golib before any fix: B never ran.
  - A Goexit raised BY A DEFERRED CALL escaped GoFrame.Run's deferred loop. The frame's earlier-deferred calls were
    skipped, so `panic("B")` was never raised, and the goroutine exited quietly where Go crashes.
  - The same skip hit a non-panicking frame. Go (go1.26.10, measured): after a deferred Goexit, the remaining deferred
    calls run, recover() reads nil in them, a running panic is abandoned, and the goroutine exits.
- Fix: GoFrame.Run catches GoexitException from a deferred call and keeps running the remaining calls, with
  RecoverablePanic cleared. The Goexit is re-raised at the tail, unless a later call raised a panic that is still
  unrecovered: that panic supersedes it, as in Go. The abandoned panic stays on the sequence.
- So B.Link DOES reach A through the Goexit's unwinding. Go prints nothing for the goexit link and no TAB after it, so
  the direct B -> A link prints Go's exact text.
- Arms, red first, all go1.26.10-measured. Negative control: with GoFrame.cs patched back to the base, 4 of the 6
  filtered arms red; restored byte-identical.
  - CrashReportChainTests, the two-panic case: "panic: A\n\tpanic: B" (A not [recovered]).
  - CrashReportChainTests, recover then Goexit: "panic: A [recovered]\n\tpanic: B".
  - CrashReportChainTests, `defer panic B; Goexit`: "panic: B" alone. It printed this before the fix as well.
  - GoFrameTests: ADeferredGoexitStillRunsTheRemainingDeferredCalls and
    ADeferredGoexitAbandonsThePanicTheFrameWasRunning.
