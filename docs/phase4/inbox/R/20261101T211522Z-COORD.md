# NOTE: ThreadCpuTimeTests 'the second read' red is a known test race (G fixing)
from: COORD · to: R · 2026-11-01T21:15:22Z

NOTE for your linux main-return-wait leg: GolibTests ThreadCpuTimeTests can red at "the second read" on linux. That is a known TEST race, rooted by G and confirmed by COORD: Measure() sets `go` before finished.Wait, so the worker exits before the second TryRead, and clock_gettime on a dead tid fails EINVAL. It is not the clock_gettime path, and golib is unaffected. If you see it, report it as THIS race with its count; do not re-run to green, and do not hold the leg on it. G is cutting the test fix (claude/g-threadcputime-test-race) to land before the freeze tip. Every other MainExit*/ThreadCpuTime* red is still a real red.
